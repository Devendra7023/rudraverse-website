Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RudraVerse handles information when you visit this website or authorize the RudraVerse YouTube publishing application (“Service”).

1. Information we access

If you choose to connect a Google account, the Service requests Google OAuth permission to access the YouTube channel features needed to upload and schedule videos. This may include your channel identity and the ability to upload videos and manage their publication status. We only intend to use this access to operate the publishing features you request.

2. How information is used

Information accessed through Google is used to connect the selected channel, upload video content, set video titles and descriptions, and schedule publication. We do not use Google user data for advertising, sell it, or use it to build advertising profiles.

3. Storage and security

OAuth credentials and access tokens are intended to be stored in restricted server-side secrets or secure configuration, accessible only to the Service operator. They must not be placed in public source-code repositories or shared publicly. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

4. Sharing

We do not sell Google user data. Data is not shared with third parties except as needed to operate the Service (for example, Google APIs and the hosting provider), to comply with law, or to protect the Service and its users.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain only information needed to operate the Service. You can disconnect the application at any time through your Google Account’s third-party connections/security settings. To request deletion of information held by the Service, contact us using the email below. Revoking access may stop automated publishing.

6. Google API Services

The Service’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Children’s privacy

This Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the Service changes. The updated date will be shown at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: rudraverse.media@gmail.com
